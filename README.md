Employee Performance Analysis Dashboard

This repository contains an Employee Performance Analysis Dashboard, designed to explore workforce structure, salary distribution, and department-wise trends. The dashboard highlights key insights that help organizations understand employee demographics, compensation balance, and areas for strategic improvement.

📁 Project Overview

The dashboard analyzes employees across departments, cities, and age groups. It provides insights about:

Salary distribution

Workforce age patterns

Department composition

City-wise salary split

Future focus areas for HR & management decisions

📌 Key Insights

🔹 1. Salary Distribution

50% of employees fall under the Medium salary category.

29% are in the High salary category.

21% fall in the Low salary bracket.
👉 Indicates a balanced pay structure, with most employees earning mid-range salaries.

🔹 2. Department Composition

Workforce distribution is fairly even across departments:

Data Analyst

Data Engineer

Data Scientist

MIS Analyst

Research Analyst

Others

Each contributes roughly 15–18% of the total workforce, suggesting a diverse and balanced talent mix.

🔹 3. Age Group Salary Trend

Junior employees have the highest total salary payout.

They are followed by Middle-aged staff.

Senior employees form the smallest share.
👉 Indicates a young workforce, likely in early to mid-career stages.

🔹 4. Salary by Department

Data Analysts and Data Engineers earn the highest total salaries.

MIS Analysts and "Other" roles earn comparatively lower totals.
👉 Suggests higher demand or compensation for analytics and engineering roles.

🔹 5. Department vs Age Group

Junior and Middle-aged employees dominate across all departments.

Senior-level presence is lower.
👉 This highlights an opportunity to invest in leadership development.

🔹 6. City-wise Salary Split

Salary distribution across cities is well balanced (15–18% each).
👉 Indicates no major geographical salary bias or an evenly distributed workforce.

📚 Overall Observations

The organization shows:

A diverse and well-structured workforce

Strong representation of mid-level analytical roles

Young employee base with potential for long-term growth

🚀 Future Focus Areas

Improve senior-level retention

Review and optimize salary differentiation

Track department-wise performance growth

Provide training and skill development to help employees adapt to changes

Reduce turnover rate through development programs
